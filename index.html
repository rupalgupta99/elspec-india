Who We Are

Elspec India

Elspec India is the Indian arm of Elspec Engineering Ltd., a global leader in power quality analysis and real-time power quality solutions since 1988. We bring Elspec's field-proven PQZIP-powered analyzers, monitoring software and compensation technology to industries, utilities and commercial establishments across India, backed by local sales, service and support out of our Indore and Delhi offices.

Our team works closely with plant engineers, facility managers and utilities to diagnose power quality issues, reduce downtime and improve energy efficiency — combining Elspec's global R&D with on-ground Indian expertise.

Vision

To be India's most trusted power quality partner, helping every industry measure, control and resolve power quality challenges.

Mission

Deliver Elspec's world-class analyzers, software and solutions with responsive local service, so Indian businesses lose zero production to preventable power quality events.

Goal

Expand power quality awareness and adoption across Indian manufacturing, utilities and commercial sectors through audits, training and reliable after-sales support.
