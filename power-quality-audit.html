How It Works

Our Audit Process

Site Assessment

Our engineers review your electrical network, load profile and known problem areas.

Instrumentation

Elspec analyzers are installed at key points to continuously record full waveform data.

Data Analysis

Recorded events and trends are analysed against IEC/IEEE power quality standards.

Action Plan

A clear report with findings, risk ranking and recommended corrective solutions.

Why Audit

What A Power Quality Audit Reveals

  • ✔Root cause of nuisance trips, equipment failures and unexplained downtime
  • ✔Voltage sags, swells, flicker and harmonic distortion across your network
  • ✔Compliance gaps against grid-code and internal power quality standards
  • ✔Opportunities for energy savings and extended equipment life
  • ✔Whether real-time compensation (e.g. the Equalizer) would resolve your issue
  • ✔A prioritised, cost-justified remediation roadmap
